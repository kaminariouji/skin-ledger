Framing matters

  1. Fill the circle with your face. Drag to move, scroll or slide to zoom.
  2. Face the camera straight on. A turned or tilted head is rejected by the model.
  3. Even light, no makeup, same room each time. The comparison is only as good as the repeat.

The framed square is sent to YouCam Skin AI through this machine's own server. The API key never reaches the browser. The selfie leaves this machine only for that call. A product-label photo is sent to OCR.space to be read.
